De-Energize & LOTO — never skip a step

  1. IDENTIFY the correct circuit & section
  2. OPEN disconnects — visible & verified open
  3. LOCK & TAG — each worker's own lock
  4. TEST for absence of voltage (test-before-touch)
  5. GROUND — apply protective grounds / EPZ

Grounding Order

Apply: ground / structure end FIRST → then the line end.
Remove: line end FIRST → ground end LAST.
  • Size grounds for the available fault current.
  • Clean contact points; clamps tight.
  • Bond an equipotential zone at the work site.
  • Use grounding sticks — treat as energized until on.

It is current, not voltage, that kills. Wet skin = far more current.

Emergency / Pole-Top Rescue

  1. Protect yourself — remove / de-energize the source first
  2. Call for help — know address & cross-streets
  3. Lower the patient (handline / rescue device)
  4. CPR + AED — minutes matter
  5. Transport for medical eval — internal injury hides
Never become the second victim.

The Second Point of Contact & the Hierarchy of Controls

Before you reach for any energized part, ask: “Where is my second point of contact?” Cover it, insulate it, or remove yourself from it.
  • Common second points: grounded neutral, pole ground, guys, crossarm hardware, another phase, the bucket, a tool, a co-worker.
  • Glove and cover: gloves protect your hands; cover-up removes the second point.
  • Cover nearest hazard first → remove in reverse (last on, first off).
CONTROLS — top down
1 Eliminate (de-energize) 2 Substitute / engineer 3 Isolate the worker 4 Administrative 5 PPE (last line)
  • Treat every conductor as energized until you prove it dead & grounded.
  • Test the tester before and after.
  • Hold a tailboard; everyone has stop-work authority.
