The Concept That Saves Lives

The Second Point of Contact

Current can only flow when it has a complete path. Touch one energized conductor while perfectly insulated from everything else and there is no circuit — that's why a bird sits unharmed on a wire.

The second point of contact — a grounded neutral, a guy wire, a crossarm, another phase, a tool, a co-worker — is what completes the circuit through you.
