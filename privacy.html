Privacy Policy for Webcast

Effective date: May 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Webcast app ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use it. Webcast is a casting companion: the Android version sends video to the Roku version, which receives the stream over your local network. We respect your privacy and have designed Webcast to collect as little information as possible.

What Webcast Does

Webcast consists of two components: an Android app that lets you select online videos to cast, and a Roku channel that connects to your Android device over your local network (using your device's local IP address) and plays the cast video on your TV. All communication between the Android app and the Roku channel happens locally on your home network. Webcast does not relay your video through any of our servers.

Information We Do Not Collect

Webcast does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information. We do not knowingly collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) including names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or payment details. We do not collect viewing history, watched URLs, IP addresses, or any other usage data.

Local Network Information

To connect your Android device to your Roku TV, the Android app discovers Roku devices on your local Wi-Fi network and exchanges a local IP address with the Roku channel. This information is used solely to establish the cast connection on your home network and is not transmitted to us or to any third party.

Information Collected by Roku

Use of the Roku channel is also subject to Roku's own privacy practices. Roku may collect information about your device and your use of Roku channels in accordance with its privacy policy at https://www.roku.com/legal/privacy-policy. We do not control and are not responsible for Roku's data collection practices.

Third-Party Video Sources

Videos cast through Webcast are streamed from their original third-party sources directly to your devices. We are not responsible for the data collection or content practices of those upstream video providers. Reviewing the privacy policies of those sites is recommended.

Children's Privacy

Webcast is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Advertising and Analytics

Webcast does not display advertisements and does not integrate with any third-party advertising networks, analytics providers, or social media services.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of Webcast after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
